Why unknown and indeterminate are separate
They fail for different reasons and a judge should be able to tell at a glance. Unknown means the law is clear and the building record is thin: we know exactly which assessor field is missing. Indeterminate means the legal record itself is incomplete or self-contradictory. Collapsing them would hide the more interesting finding.
Accessibility
No state is carried by hue alone. Each has a distinct glyph, a word, and a different border treatment: solid, dashed, dotted, double. The blue and ochre families differ in lightness as well as hue, so they separate in greyscale and for the roughly one in twelve men with a color vision deficiency. Nothing uses red against green.
Integration requirement
The engine currently emits five result values. This design needs two more signals it may not yet carry: a boolean separating indeterminate from unknown, and, for every uncertain result, a structured missing-fact pair rather than prose. Flagged for reconciliation once Codex commits.
