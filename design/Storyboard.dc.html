0:00 · Problem
"Housing law is layered: state, then city, each with its own coverage tests and dates. The right answer depends on the exact address and the exact day. In this scoring a missed rule costs double and an honest unknown earns partial credit, so the incentive and the ethics point the same way."
0:25 · Architecture, clicks 1 and 2
"The model extracts. Deterministic code decides. Here is the rule card: plain language on top, the machine-readable coverage test underneath. Nothing a judge sees was decided by a language model."
0:55 · The unknown, clicks 3 and 4
"This rule is in force. It turns on a fact the assessor record does not contain for this building. We say which fact, and what would resolve it. Click through and the exact sentence of law is highlighted where it sits in the document. The match is verified at render time."
1:40 · Change tracking, click 5
"One parameter moves. Three shapes come out of it: a law whose date arrives, a bill that is not yet law and can never resolve to applies, and a measure that failed and must never appear in any lookup."
2:10 · What we could not do, click 6
"Two sources we could not obtain. The system reports those rules as indeterminate rather than filling the hole. Two independent readers, one Claude and one GPT, extracted the same documents blind. We published their disagreements instead of reconciling them quietly. None was a fabrication."
2:40 · Evidence Debt
"Every unknown you saw is a row in a ledger over these [N] addresses. Grouped by city, it shows which single missing field blocks the most buildings. We set out to answer questions about housing law. We also measured where the public record cannot answer them."

FALLBACK · FOUR TIERS · ONE DECISION RULE
Decide the tier at T minus 10 minutes with a full six-click smoke test, then never change it mid-talk except downward. Never debug in front of a judge.
Tier 2 · PrimaryNO NETWORK
Real UI over frozen validated output files and shipped source documents, served from local disk. Nothing calls a model or a geocoder. Trigger to drop: any checkpoint over two seconds, or any card rendering a value not in the validated files.

QUESTIONS TO EXPECT, AND THE HONEST ANSWER
"How do you know the extraction is right?"
Two independent readers, one Claude and one GPT, extracted the same documents blind. Disagreements are published, not reconciled away. None was fabrication; all were about how to represent uncertainty.
"Is extraction really automated?"
One generic prompt, one command, no per-document code. Show the audit log, not the source.
"Why so many unknowns?"
Because the assessor data lacks unit counts and build years for whole cities. Naming that is the honest result; filling it would be the failure.
"Can you verify the corpus is untampered?"
No. The manifest hashes match nothing in the distributed text and their scope is unknown. We record our own hash and say so on screen.
WHAT THIS DESIGN NEEDS FROM THE BACKEND
P1A machine-readable flag separating indeterminate from unknown. Without it the two most interesting cards collapse into one.
P2A structured missing fact plus reason, not prose, so the card renders "missing" and "resolves if" as fields.
P3Per-address prior and current state in the change output, for the struck-through diff line.
—Withheld propagation as unknown with reason and needs_fact is reported built. Provisional until audited; nothing binds to it yet.
—Character offsets are optional. Verified span text plus source id is sufficient; the drawer locates by exact match, which it must do anyway to prove the span is real.
—Evidence Debt asks for nothing. It is a client-side grouping of the validated unknown and indeterminate results over the evaluated address set, and no other.
The field-level binding map, written by semantic role rather than field name, lives in the project doc "Demo run sheet and data-binding map." Final binding waits for the Codex commit, the Sonnet audit, and Sol's validated outputs, in that order.
