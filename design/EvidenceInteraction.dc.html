THE DERIVED VARIANT
When the claim is derived rather than stated, the drawer shows two panes stacked: the span that was read, and under it the rule that was applied to it. Never one pane implying the document said the answer.

MOTION SPEC, DELIBERATELY SMALL
180msDrawer slides in from the right, ease-out. Nothing else on screen moves.
120msHighlight fades up after the drawer settles, so the eye lands on it last. This single delay is what makes the span feel found rather than pre-staged.
0msCard state changes on the as-of toggle. Instant. A status flip must not look animated or a judge will suspect theatre.
noneNo skeleton shimmer, no spinner, no count-up numbers, no scroll hijacking.
Everything honors prefers-reduced-motion, which cuts all three to zero. The demo must be legible with motion off, because that is how it will be screen-recorded.
Keyboard and focus, final
Tab order: address, date A, date B, Look up, then each card's source link in reading order, then the ruled-out toggle. Enter on a source link opens the drawer as a labelled modal dialog with focus on its heading; Tab cycles inside it; Escape closes and returns focus to the link that opened it. The as-of toggle is a two-button radio group: arrow keys switch, and the change is announced. Focus rings are 2px, high contrast, never suppressed.

When the span will not match
The drawer opens anyway and states plainly that the quoted text could not be located in the distributed source, naming the document. It never silently shows a nearby paragraph.
This should never fire: the pipeline drops unverified spans. It exists so that if it ever does, the failure is loud.
Summary-sourced evidence
Where the only text we hold is a retrieved summary rather than source law, the pane is hatched and labelled as such, and the claim above it carries lowered confidence.

to masquerade as a quotation.
Integrity line
The drawer footer records our own hash of the distributed text and states that the manifest hash scope is unknown, so no integrity claim is made from it.

EMPTY STATE
An address with no rules in the result set is never a blank panel. It states which jurisdictions were searched, how many rules were tested, and why each was ruled out, with the ruled-out list one click away.
"[n] rules tested across [jurisdictions]. None covers this building. [n] were ruled out on [predicate], [n] on [predicate]."
UNRESOLVED ADDRESS
If the geocoder could not place the address, the app stops at the jurisdiction panel and says so. It does not fall back to the mailing city and keep going. Showing the stop is better than showing a wrong answer confidently.
This is worth demoing deliberately if time allows. It is a 10-second proof of the central discipline.
