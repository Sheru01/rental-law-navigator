Evidence chain

Seven rungs from answer to source

Always rendered as one vertical ladder on one screen. No tabs, no accordions above rung five. A judge should be able to read top to bottom in about eight seconds.
01 ANSWER
[the one-sentence answer]
[STATE CHIP] · as of [date]
02 WHY
The deciding fact, named. Never "based on applicable law". The sentence must contain the field and its value, for example the unit count the test turned on, or the one that was absent from the assessor record.
03 RULE
[rule.title] [team_rule_id]
[citation]
coverage: [predicate] [op] [value]
needs_fact: [list, or none]
04 EXACT TEXT
VERBATIM SPAN, MACHINE-VERIFIED AGAINST THE SOURCE FILE
[the exact sentence from the source document, highlighted in place]
Substring match confirmed at render time. A span that fails this check never ships.
05 SOURCE
doc[doc_id] · [official | code publisher | secondary]
url[source_url]
retrieved[retrieved_at]
integritylocal text hash recorded; manifest hash scope unknown, so no integrity claim is made
06 EPISTEMICS
STATED The source text says it, and rung 04 proves it. Filled chip.
DERIVED Follows mechanically from stated text plus a named rule. The chip expands to name that rule. A derived date never renders as a stated one.
INFERRED Plausible, not established by the source alone. Carries its basis inline.
UNKNOWN The source does not supply it. Renders as an empty slot with the gap named, never as a blank or a dash.
07 TIME
EFFECTIVE
[date or null]
[stated | derived | not establishable]
AS OF
[query date]
user-controlled, never the clock
RETRIEVED
[capture date]
the law may have moved since
THE TEST
If a judge can point at any sentence on the answer screen and reach its verbatim source in one click, the provenance claim is real. If any sentence cannot be reached that way, it should not be on the screen. That is the acceptance criterion for this design, and it is checkable in the demo itself.
